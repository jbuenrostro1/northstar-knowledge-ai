# Northstar Knowledge — Project Overview

## Company Background

Northstar Software is a fictional B2B SaaS company with approximately 50 employees. Northstar provides a customer support operations platform designed for small and midsize businesses.

The platform helps growing support teams centralize and manage customer requests through features such as ticket management, shared support inboxes, customer profiles, knowledge bases, reporting, integrations, and workflow automation.


## Business Problem

Northstar's Customer Support team is currently experiencing difficulty finding consistent answers to recurring customer issues. Currently, Support Specialists rely on communicating with one another through Slack to find solutions. 
The existing documentation is disorganized, and different team members may use different approaches to resolve the same issue. As a result, information retrieval is inefficient, and customer support can be inconsistent.


## Project Objective

This project will provide Northstar's Customer Support team with an AI-assisted interface for finding approved internal documentation more efficiently, receiving consistent answers, and identifying exactly where the information came from.


## Project Scope

For V1, Northstar Knowledge is designed for the Customer Support team and will search approved internal support documentation. If a Support Specialist asks a question and the AI assistant cannot find sufficient information within the approved 
documentation to answer it, the assistant will acknowledge the limitation and recommend escalation rather than generate an unsupported answer.

Live customer or account data from external systems, such as a CRM or database, is outside the scope of V1.
