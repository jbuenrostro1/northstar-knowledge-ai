## FAQ-001 — How do I reset my password?

If a customer forgets their password, instruct them to follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the Northstar login page.
2. Select **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter the email address associated with their Northstar account.
4. Follow the instructions provided in the password-reset email.
5. Create a new password.
6. Return to the login page and sign in using the new password.


## FAQ-002 — How do I downgrade my subscription?

If a customer wants to downgrade their subscription, instruct them to follow these steps:

1. Navigate to **Account → Subscriptions → Change Subscription**.
2. Review the available subscription tiers and select the desired lower tier.
3. Review the features and benefits that will no longer be available after downgrading.

### Important Subscription Information

- Customers retain their current subscription benefits until the end of the current billing cycle.
- The downgrade takes effect at the beginning of the next billing cycle.
